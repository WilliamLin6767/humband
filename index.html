How it works

  1. Pitch tracking

    The YIN algorithm measures your pitch 100 times a second, then works out how sharp or flat you hum overall.

  2. Notes

    Pitch jumps and small dips in volume mark where notes start. Each note snaps to the nearest 16th and to the key.

  3. Chords

    A hidden Markov model picks chords that fit your notes and flow into each other, including the loop back to the top.

  4. Groove

    The drums put kicks where you started notes, and the bass locks to the kick. Neural drums use Google's Magenta model.

Your recordings never leave your device.
